Overview

The short version

Epidex has no login, no account, and no server of its own — there is nowhere for your watch history to be sent, because Epidex has nothing to send it to. Two things do touch an outside party: the ads that keep the app free, and a purchase if you choose to remove them. Both are covered below in full.

Your library

What Epidex stores, and where

Everything you track — titles, progress, ratings, notes, tags, tier lists, custom covers — is written to a database that lives only on your device. Epidex has no backend, so there is no copy of your library anywhere else, and no way for anyone, including the developer, to see it.

Backups are something you start, not something that happens automatically. The Export as JSON and Export as CSV actions in Settings write a file to a location you choose, using Android's own file picker — Epidex never uploads it anywhere on your behalf. Restoring works the same way, from a file you select.

Advertising

How the ad banner works

Epidex shows a small banner ad, served by Google AdMob, on the My List and Discover screens only. To request an ad, AdMob uses your device's advertising ID — a resettable identifier, not your name, email, or anything else that identifies you personally.

If you're in the EU, EEA, UK, or Switzerland, Epidex asks for your consent — through Google's own consent prompt — before any ad request that uses that identifier for personalization. You can decline and still see ads; they'll simply be less tailored.

Google's own use of this data is governed by their policies, not Epidex's: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Purchases

The Supporter purchase

Epidex offers a one-time, optional purchase — Supporter — that removes ads and unlocks a few extras. It's handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Epidex never sees your payment method, card number, or billing address; Google handles the transaction and simply tells the app "purchased" or not. That result is stored on your device so the unlock still works offline.

Show & movie data

Where the show, movie and anime data comes from

Searching or browsing sends your search text to whichever of these public catalogues can answer it. None of them receive your name, an account identifier, or anything else about you — just the query, the way a web search would work.

SourceUsed for
TVmazeTV shows, episodes, air dates
ShikimoriAnime metadata and schedules
Jikan (MyAnimeList)Anime fallback and enrichment
WikidataMovies, with no account or key needed

TMDB and TheTVDB are opt-in: Epidex works fully without them, and only contacts them at all if you've pasted your own key into Settings.

Your control

Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, deleting it is entirely in your hands:

  • Uninstalling the app removes your library and every preference along with it.
  • Clearing app storage in Android's system settings does the same, without a full uninstall.
  • Removing a title from within Epidex deletes its progress, tags, and episode history immediately.

There is no account to delete and no server-side copy to request the removal of, because none exists.

Children

Children's privacy

Epidex is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone in that age group. Because the app collects no personal information at all beyond the advertising identifier described above, this is largely moot — but it's stated here for clarity.
